The pitch

Ryan is a data and AI engineer who is also a working musician — a lifelong pianist with 20+ years of studio production and DJ performance, and the author of RML SetMaster, an open-source DJ analytics tool that joins Traktor XML to Spotify metadata. On the engineering side he was COO and primary systems architect through an e-commerce brand's $300K-to-$30M growth, then built a $20K-MRR analytics product at an AI SaaS company, shipping AI-native with Claude Code, Codex and MCP. For a music, audio or creator-tools company he's the rare hire who is both the engineer and the customer — and he's looking for that role full-time, or as one dedicated engagement.

Why this vertical

The engineer and the customer

I am a paying expert user of the tools these companies make — Native Instruments, Ableton, Steinberg, Universal Audio, Reason Studios, Arturia, SSL, RME and Focusrite hardware and software, professionally, for 20+ years. I know the workflow from the inside, and I have shipped software to that workflow, which means I can sit with product, data, and the user at the same time.
