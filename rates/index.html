Pilot project · 30-day engagement

What does a pilot project look like?

A pilot is a 30-day working engagement. You pay $500 to start, and nothing else until the 30 days are up.

Kickoff & rate

$500kickoff fee

  • $500 to get started. The kickoff fee covers your first 6 hours.
  • $85 / hour after that — the pilot rate, billed once at the end of the 30 calendar days.
  • You set the ceiling. Cap the hours, name a deliverable, or both. Past the 6 hours the kickoff covers, there is no minimum.
Recommended pace

10–20hours per week

  • 10–20 hours per week is the recommendation — the range where a significant deliverable or a working system actually gets built inside the window.
  • What that looks like: 10 hours per week for 30 days works out to about $2,900 billed at the end.
After the 30 days

Day 31 is an open door.

Roll into a project tier, keep going hourly as needed, or stop — no obligation.

How engagements start

The 30-minute conversation

Unbilled. You describe your problem; I listen — and you leave knowing exactly what working together would look like. And what it isn’t: no pitch deck, no pressure. The prices are already on this page.

  1. Intro call — unbilled

    ~30 minutes. You describe your needs or project; I listen. No charge, no obligation.

  2. Proposal & SOW

    I pitch a tier — or a phased sequence from a lower tier to a higher one — with a scope of work we iterate on together, transparently.

  3. Final negotiation

    Price, deliverables, and timeline are finalized together. There’s always a conversation to be had.

  4. Kickoff

    Your time gets blocked on the calendar and the work begins.
