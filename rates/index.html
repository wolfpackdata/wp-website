How engagements start

The 30-minute conversation

Unbilled. You describe your problem; I listen — and you leave knowing exactly what working together would look like. And what it isn’t: no pitch deck, no pressure. The prices are already on this page.

  1. Intro call — unbilled

    ~30 minutes. You describe your needs or project; I listen. No charge, no obligation.

  2. Proposal & SOW

    I pitch a tier — or a phased sequence from a lower tier to a higher one — with a scope of work we iterate on together, transparently.

  3. Final negotiation

    Price, deliverables, and timeline are finalized together. There’s always a conversation to be had.

  4. Kickoff

    Your time gets blocked on the calendar and the work begins.
