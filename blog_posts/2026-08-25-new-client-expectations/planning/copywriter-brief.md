**For:** the person writing the post. **Not** the post itself.

exists at [`first-draft.md`](first-draft.md), built from Ry's dictated transcript at his

treat its suggestions as suggestions. The transcript itself and all fact-check notes are

**Deliverables:** `raw-blog-post.md` and `raw-linkedin-post.md` in the post folder

## 1. Hard constraints

- **Facts may not change.** Every number, claim, and named tool comes from the draft and
  `source-notes.md`. Do not round, inflate, or invent.
- **No measured client outcome, percentage, or multiple, ever.** The ROI section describes
  the *method* of measuring; it may not claim a result. One invented number would
  discredit the four case studies on the site.
- **No client named** beyond HBO and Time Warner, which Ry cleared for the early-career
  mention. No invented testimonial.
- **Links:** the AI Command case study (`/wolfpack-ai-command/`) and the ROI calculator
  (`/roi-calculator/`) are public and encouraged. **Never link** the pilot-project, hire,
  or github pages — they are noindex, direct-link only.
- **AI Command never implies replacing project managers** — "bespoke project manager and a
  developer's best friend" is the safe framing; stay on that side.
- **The BigQuery cost passage keeps the hedge** — "$50 to $100 a month, and it is often
  less." The fact-check behind it is in `source-notes.md`; do not restore the transcript's
  "lowest level of storage" rationale, which was checked and dropped.
- **One CTA destination: the 30-minute intro call.** Exact URL confirmed at push time.

## 3. Traps

- **This post lists ~ten offerings; do not let it become ten mini-sections of equal
  weight.** The safety mentality and the honesty sections are the differentiators; the
  tooling inventory supports them.
- **Do not soften the exclusions.** "I don't have any enterprise experience" is doing more
  selling than any capability claim in the piece.
- **Do not promise what the mechanism doesn't.** The email guardrail claim is "agents
  draft, a human sends" — not "AI can never send email." Keep claims at the strength of
  the described mechanism.
- **The data-training question** (feedback item 6) needs Ry's answer before anything is
  written about it. Ask; do not assume.
